Privacy Policy for Tomorrow

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Tomorrow is a personal task-planning app made by PPPProdukcija. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Data you create

The tasks, plans, and progress you enter in Tomorrow are stored on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, synced through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to your other devices. This data is tied to your Apple ID and is not visible to us — we do not run our own servers and do not have access to the contents of your tasks or plans.

Calendar access

If you grant calendar permission, Tomorrow reads your existing calendar events so it can show which days are already busy. It only reads this information to display it inside the app — it never creates, edits, or deletes anything in your calendar, and calendar data is never sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Tomorrow uses them to remind you about planned tasks. Notifications are scheduled and delivered on your device; no notification content is collected by us.

Purchases

Tomorrow offers an optional subscription through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Payment is handled entirely by Apple — Tomorrow never sees or stores your payment details. We only receive confirmation from Apple of whether a subscription is active, so the app can unlock the corresponding features.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We don't collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't have a user account system or our own backend — your data lives on your device and in your own iCloud account.

Children's privacy

Tomorrow is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
